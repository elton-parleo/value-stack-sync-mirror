# Restore the customer deck’s mobile visual storytelling

## Goal
Rebuild the phone experience so it carries the same visual impact, pacing, and authored storytelling as the desktop presentation. Preserve every word and claim.

## What will change
- Audit all 13 desktop slides against their mobile sections and map every meaningful image, logo, diagram, product visual, chart, and interface composition.
- Replace text-only mobile substitutes with responsive, mobile-native versions of the original visual stories.
- Reuse the deck’s actual visual assets where practical, including merchant and platform marks, product imagery, charts, interface panels, and photography.
- Recompose wide diagrams and dense comparisons into stacked sequences that remain readable without losing relationships or hierarchy.
- Introduce stronger visual pacing between sections through deliberate dark/light shifts, full-width imagery, data-led compositions, and editorial crops.
- Keep the current vertical-scroll interaction, compact header, desktop-view option, complete content, and private search blocking.
- Leave the desktop presentation unchanged.
