# Humanize the Insights library

## Goal
Make all nine Insights articles feel more conversational, authored, and story-led without changing their research, claims, SEO targets, or Parleo’s confident founder voice.

## What will change
- Refine openings so each article enters through a concrete observation, question, or moment rather than generic framing.
- Vary sentence length and paragraph rhythm while keeping the writing concise and executive-friendly.
- Remove repeated scaffolding such as “What this means,” mechanical summaries, forced contrasts, and stock transitions where they weaken the flow.
- Add light connective tissue between evidence, examples, and conclusions so the argument reads as one story rather than a sequence of data blocks.
- Keep first-person founder language only where it already fits the evidence and established voice.
- Conservatively polish titles, descriptions, takeaways, FAQs, headings, captions, and article prose where needed.

## Guardrails
- Preserve every fact, statistic, named example, citation, backlink, caveat, sequence, and level of certainty.
- Do not invent anecdotes, opinions, or personality.
- Keep all visual blocks, article structure, URLs, dates, metadata, and SEO keywords intact.
- Do not use em dashes or en dashes in site copy.
- Remove invisible characters and accidental formatting residue if found.

## Validation
- Compare the revised copy against the source article by article for factual and tonal drift.
- Confirm all nine posts remain registered and renderable.
- Run focused validation and the production build.
