# parleo.io copy deck

v1 · Oct 25, 2026 · locked from storyboard rounds 1 to 3 (Samar's picks)

The single source for site copy. Headlines and body are final unless marked open. Image lines are briefs for the hero and section rounds, built to the visual idea "the agent's-eye view" in `20261023_parleo_site_design_rules_v2.md`.

## Company line

**The agentic commerce platform for brands and retailers.**
Parleo makes sure every AI answer gets your products, prices and promotions right, and shows you what that's worth.

## Home

**01 · Hero**
**Your next customer just asked an AI what to buy.**
Parleo makes sure the answer is you. We help brands and retailers get their products, prices and promotions right in every AI answer, so more of those recommendations go your way.
Image: one product on a stone plinth under hard light, an AI answer card pinned to it by a hairline, the quoted price resolving from grey to the member price in blue as the page loads.

**02 · The shift**
**There is no page two.**
Shoppers used to search, read reviews, browse the shelf and click through to your page. Now they ask an AI and get one answer with one or two products in it. AI-driven retail traffic is up 393% in a year, and it converts about 42% better.
Image: a shopper in a sunlit kitchen with her phone, her question set above her in mono, the old funnel folding down into one answer card that names a single product.
Source: Adobe, Q1 2026.

**03 · The gap**
**AI prices your best customers like strangers.**
Your most loyal customer and a first-time visitor get the same answer from AI: full price, no perks. Everything you built to win that customer disappears at the moment they decide.
Image: a white leather sneaker on a plinth, tagged "what AI quotes, $104.99" in grey and "what a new member pays, $85.00" in blue.
Source: adidas public prices and adiClub terms, Sep 29, 2026.

**04 · Mentioned vs. recommended**
**Being mentioned isn't the same as being recommended.**
Most brands show up somewhere in AI answers. Far fewer get recommended, and fewer still at the right price. Parleo tracks all three every week, so you can see exactly where the sale slips away.
Image: a shelf shot dead straight, most products carrying a small grey "mentioned" tag, one carrying the blue "recommended" tag. Pinned proof: a brand named in 172 of 180 answers, told to wait for a sale in 26 of 32.
Source: Gap Inc., Oct 21, 2026.

**05 · Brands**
**Make the money you already spend show up in the answer.**
Brands spend more than $200 billion a year on trade promotion to win the shelf, and almost none of it reaches an AI answer. Parleo gets your coupons, rebates and rewards into the answer at every retailer that sells you.
Image: a store endcap with a brand's shelf-talker in frame, a hairline to an answer card reading "no coupon applies", stamped wrong, the real coupon beside it in blue.
Source: Strategy& / PwC. Kimberly-Clark case, Sep 29, 2026.

**06 · Retailers**
**Your loyalty program is the advantage agents can't copy.**
AI can't see your member prices, points or perks, so it sends your members wherever looks cheapest. Parleo puts that value into every answer, so you win at full price.
Image: a member card on a walnut counter, a ledger resolving list price to the member's price in blue, and a pinned stat: told she's a member, the agent picks the retailer 55 times out of 55.
Source: Gap Inc., Oct 21, 2026.

**07 · The platform**
**Parleo scores, tracks and optimizes how AI agents recommend you.**
See how every major AI assistant describes, ranks and prices you, fix what it gets wrong, and put your best offers into the answer. Then watch what it's worth, week by week.
Image: a long band of four product photographs, each carrying one rebuilt console moment (the score, an answer beside the live page, one product sent to six AI channels, the dollar readout), threaded by one hairline.

**08 · Evidence**
**This is what AI is telling your customers right now.**
Wrong prices, missing member deals, coupons it claims don't exist. Here are real examples from brands you know, and the fix for each.
Image: six product still lifes, each with a brand logo, the agent's words clipped to it and the truth stamped beside. Tap one and the fixed answer replaces the wrong one in blue.
Cases: Petco, adidas, Gap, Ulta Beauty, Nordstrom, Kimberly-Clark. Public examples, not clients: copy says "the fix", never "we fixed it for them".

**09 · Versus visibility tools**
**Visibility gets you into the answer. Value gets you picked.**
Visibility tools tell you whether AI mentions you. Parleo makes sure that when it does, it has your right price and your best offer, because that's what wins the sale.
Image: the depth diagram. Mentioned on top, then priced right, then offer included, then chosen, with Parleo's blue running the full depth.

**10 · Close**
**See what AI says about your brand.**
Get a free Agentic Value Audit: how AI assistants describe, rank and price you against your competitors, and the fixes worth making first. Public data only, ready in days.
Image: a store after hours, one product lit on a counter, an empty answer card that fills with the visitor's brand name as they type.

## Product

**01 · Hero**
**The platform for winning AI recommendations.**
See how AI agents describe you, fix what they get wrong, and put your best offers in every answer. Built for brands and retailers, with the revenue impact tracked every week.
Image: a hand setting a product on a pale stone counter, six agent tags pinned around it, each ticking blue as it's verified.

**02 · Measure**
**One number for how AI treats your brand.**
Share of Algorithm rolls visibility, access and true value into a score out of 100, tracked weekly against your competitors.
Image: the score set on a shelf photograph, 47 of 100 with three pillar bars. Sample account, labeled.

**03 · Answers**
**Every answer, word for word, next to the truth.**
See exactly what AI told your shoppers, next to what your own site said that day. Every wrong price, outdated product or missing offer is flagged and ranked by what it costs you.
Image: an answer card clipped to a bag of dog food on a shelf, the wrong price struck through in grey, the real first-order price pinned in blue.

**04 · Access**
**Agents can't recommend what they can't read.**
Many sites block AI assistants without knowing it, or bury prices where agents can't find them. Parleo finds every gap and shows you the fix.
Image: a shop door at night, a column of agent names in mono stamped 403, one turning to 200 in blue.

**05 · TrueSync**
**One accurate version of every product, everywhere agents look.**
TrueSync keeps one complete, checked version of every product, price and offer, and sends it everywhere AI agents read, from Google and ChatGPT to your own site. A week later, it checks the answers to confirm they changed.
Image: a single product on a plinth, a hairline fanning to six channel tags, each marked published then verified.
Open: present tense. The demo mocks publishing and the live build doesn't publish yet. Decide the tense before launch.

**06 · Value layer**
**Win on value instead of discounts.**
Show AI the full value of buying from you, from member pricing to free shipping, so it stops sending shoppers to the next sale. You set the rules, and test them before anything goes live.
Image: a loyalty card on a counter, a ledger resolving to the member's price, three rule switches, a backtest chip: 0 of 9 today, 7 of 9 with the rules on. Sample, labeled.

**07 · Funded offers**
**Trade spend you can finally trace into AI answers.**
See whether the promotions you fund actually reach AI answers, at every retailer that carries you. One view the brand and the retailer can both act on.
Image: a bag of dog food beside its shelf-talker, a hairline to a tag reading "brand-funded first-order price: not in the answer", and the $9.49 Petco was really cheaper by, in blue.
Source: Petco case, Sep 28, 2026.

**08 · Fix and approve**
**Every fix ranked by what it earns.**
Every issue becomes a fix with an owner and a dollar value. Pricing, loyalty and legal each approve their part before anything goes live.
Image: three fix cards on a desk beside a fountain pen, each with owner initials and a dollar value, an approval chain ticking across.

**09 · Prove**
**Dollars, not dashboards.**
See what wrong answers cost you, what fixing them wins back, and what's changed since you started. Your team gets a weekly readout, ready to forward.
Image: a printed weekly readout on a linen table, a waterfall from total sales to revenue at stake drawn over it, marked modeled.

**10 · Brand and content**
**One version of your brand, the way agents read it.**
Your claims, sizes and product details, settled in one place and approved by your teams before AI repeats them.
Image: a skincare jar on wet stone, four shopper questions pinned to it, each with a checked answer and its source page.

**11 · Works with**
**Works with every agent and every protocol.**
ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude and Google AI Mode. Merchant Center, ACP, UCP, MCP and your own site. Nothing personal leaves your systems.
Image: none. Agent and protocol logos, small and aligned on a hairline grid.
